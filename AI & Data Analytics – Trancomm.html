AI & Data Analytics

Transforming raw information into strategic foresight and operational intelligence.

Raw data is abundant but underused. Trancomm applies machine learning, forensic methodologies and computer vision to turn complex data streams into actionable intelligence, stronger security and automated operational efficiency.

Data Analytics

Engineer data pipelines that aggregate, sanitize and analyze organizational information into one useful view.

  • Predictive analytics Forecast trends and operational bottlenecks.
  • Business intelligence Deliver visual dashboards for decision-makers.
  • Data architecture Structure siloed information into secure data lakes.
  • Process optimization Identify inefficiencies and model better workflows.

Digital Forensics

Apply rigorous frameworks to uncover the truth hidden within digital footprints after incidents or anomalies.

  • Incident response Isolate compromised segments and trace attack vectors.
  • E-discovery Preserve evidence with a sound chain of custody.
  • Data recovery Reconstruct data after ransomware or hardware failure.
  • Threat hunting Search telemetry for dormant or hidden threats.

AI Analysis

Deploy enterprise-grade AI to automate cognitive tasks and uncover patterns at scale.

  • Machine learning Improve resource allocation with custom models.
  • Anomaly detection Flag fraud, risk and unauthorized access.
  • Language processing Extract insight from unstructured text.
  • Decision support Accelerate complex approval workflows.

Intelligent Video Analytics

Turn passive cameras into proactive, intelligent sensors through computer vision and physical security integration.

  • Perimeter defense Distinguish genuine intrusions from harmless movement.
  • Biometric access Verify authorized users in high-security zones.
  • Behavior analytics Monitor density, bottlenecks and unusual activity.
  • PSIM integration Feed video metadata into command platforms.
