Digital Forensics Training

When a breach occurs, rapid and accurate investigation dictates the scale of the damage. We train your internal IT and security teams to act as expert first responders.

  • First Responder Incident Handling Training staff to isolate compromised systems without destroying volatile data crucial for analysis.
  • Chain of Custody & Compliance Rigorous instruction on acquiring and documenting digital evidence to ensure admissibility in legal proceedings.
  • Network & Endpoint Forensics Advanced techniques for tracing unauthorized access and recovering hidden artifacts across enterprise networks.
  • Malware Analysis Specialized training for analysts to safely detonate and reverse-engineer malicious software within sandboxed environments.
