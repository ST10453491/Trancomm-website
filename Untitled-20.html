Core Telecommunications Services

Carrier-Grade Network Infrastructure

We design, supply, and implement robust core and edge networking solutions tailored for telecommunications providers. Partnering with global leaders like Cisco and Huawei, we deploy high-capacity routers, switches, and optical transport networks (OTN).

  • IP/MPLS Core Network design and deployment
  • Data center networking and virtualization
  • SDN & Network Functions Virtualization (NFV)

Broadband & Fiber Optic (FTTx)

Trancomm is a trusted partner in rolling out large-scale broadband initiatives. We provide end-to-end optical network solutions that deliver gigabit speeds to homes, businesses, and government facilities.

  • FTTH, FTTB, and FTTT architectures
  • Passive Optical Network (PON) & GPON deployments
  • Route surveying, civil works, and fiber splicing

Command & Network Operations (NOC/SOC)

Uptime is the currency of the telecom industry. Leveraging advanced PSIM platforms like Octopus, we build state-of-the-art Network Operations Centers and Security Operations Centers.

  • Real-time network topology visualization & fault management
  • Predictive maintenance alerts via AI analytics
  • Unified monitoring for IT and remote tower facilities

Mobile Network Backhaul & RAN Optimization

To support the explosive growth of mobile data, cell towers require ultra-reliable backhaul connections. Trancomm delivers wireless and wired backhaul solutions that ensure seamless data flow from the Radio Access Network (RAN) to the core network.

  • High-capacity microwave link design and installation
  • Cell site router configuration and deployment
  • Spectrum optimization and interference mitigation strategies

Enterprise Unified Communications

Beyond infrastructure, we empower telcos to offer premium Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) to their enterprise clients, driving additional recurring revenue streams.

  • Secure, scalable VoIP and IP telephony systems
  • High-definition video conferencing & collaboration suites
  • Secure multi-tenant cloud communication environments
