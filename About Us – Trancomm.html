Who we are

Trancom IT (Pty) Ltd

Trancomm Information Technology and Services is a 100 percent (%) Black Owned and Managed company founded in early 2009. The company offers information and telecommunication (IT) solutions, strategy and advisory services that genuinely meet the needs of clients while keeping them at the forefront of rapidly changing ICT technologies.

TRANCOMM IT core services are Telecommunications, Information Technology (IT), Business Consulting and Strategy, Bespoke Solutions, as well as Solution Support and Maintenance.

Through its strategic sourcing of partners, both locally and internationally, the business is able to successfully execute and support customer-oriented ICT/IT solutions in both the private and public sectors within the SADEC borders.
